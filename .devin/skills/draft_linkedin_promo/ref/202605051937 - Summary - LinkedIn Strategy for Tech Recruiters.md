# Summary: LinkedIn Strategy to Attract Recruiters

*Source: [Freedom Freelancing](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ozwmlFencJI)*

## Core Thesis

Doing less on LinkedIn can land you more jobs. Instead of competing with hundreds of applicants on job postings, optimize your profile to make recruiters find and message you directly through a three-step strategy: LinkedIn SEO, landing page conversion, and viral content posting.

## Three-Step Strategy

### Step 1: LinkedIn SEO

Recruiters use LinkedIn search 90% of the time to find tech freelancers and IT contractors. Optimize your profile to appear in the top 10-30 search results.

**Keyword Placement (4 locations):**
- Header
- Bio/About section
- Job titles
- Work experience descriptions

**Critical Rule:** Use the **exact same keyword** consistently across all four locations. Don't vary the terminology (e.g., don't mix "database engineer," "database administrator," and "DBA").

### Step 2: Landing Page Conversion

Transform your LinkedIn profile into a landing page that converts visitors into clients.

**Three Required Elements:**

| Element | Purpose | Implementation |
|---------|---------|----------------|
| **Measurable Metrics** | Show results, not just responsibilities | Include percentages, cost reductions, profit increases (e.g., "increased sales performance by 20%", "reduced costs by 50%") |
| **Portfolio** | Technical proof of capabilities | Link to dashboards, websites, apps, or GitHub in your header; use dummy projects if real work is confidential |
| **Referrals** | Social proof from colleagues/managers | Request referrals from current/former managers and colleagues; offer to write one in return |

**Why Metrics Matter:** Google research shows people who include measurable metrics in LinkedIn profiles and resumes have a higher chance of getting hired.

### Step 3: Viral LinkedIn Posts

Write simple viral posts using the **Hook-Rehook-Body-Value-CTA framework**:

1. **Hook** - Stop the scroll with a question, bold statement, or impressive statistic
2. **Rehook** - Expand on the hook to trigger more curiosity
3. **Body** - Deliver the meat: story, lesson, resource list, or tutorial
4. **Value** - The core content that helps your audience
5. **CTA** - Call to action: ask for DMs, consultation bookings, reposts, or engagement questions

**Benefits of Posting:**
- Pushes your profile higher in search results
- Increases profile visits from LinkedIn feed (not just search)
- Builds credibility and authority
- Enables higher rates for the same work

## Success Examples

- **Valerie**: Used this framework to write viral Tableau posts, landed a freelance data analyst job within 2.5 months, got reposted by official Tableau community
- **Damian**: Implemented the strategy after a master class and wrote a LinkedIn post that went completely viral
- **100+ tech professionals** coached using this strategy had their inboxes flooded with recruiter messages for higher-paid jobs without applying

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Having empty work experience sections
- Using different keywords for the same role across profile sections
- Stating only responsibilities without measurable outcomes
- Missing portfolio links
- No referrals or social proof

## Key Insight

The old way (applying to every job, using Fiverr/Upwork, sending cold messages) is proactive work with no control over outcomes. The new way is passive attraction where recruiters come to you based on profile optimization and content authority.
