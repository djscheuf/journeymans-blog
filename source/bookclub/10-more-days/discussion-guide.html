Discussion guide

Think it over before we meet.

Our live discussion runs about 20 minutes, so there are just four questions. Before we meet, spend a few minutes on each and jot a few lines. Rough notes are plenty. Bring them along, and we will talk through as many as time allows.

Prompts and context

What goes into the prompt, and what should surround it.

  1. How did treating prompts as part of a workflow change what you put in them?

    Days 2–3: Before You Prompt, Standardize Your Prompt

    What is in your prompts now that was not there before? What did you cut?

  2. What other context artifacts might you need, even if you have not written them yet?

    Day 4: Capture Context Artifacts

    Beyond what you stored on Day 4: what would your agent have to guess without it?

Workflow and process

How the steps connect, and where else this could apply.

  1. How does the concept of ‘intermediates’ change how you might structure your workflow?

    Days 4–5: Capture Context Artifacts, Map Your SDLC

    Intermediates are the results you keep outside the chat. Which step should hand off to the next?

  2. What other processes might you map in order to augment them with AI?

    Day 5: Map Your SDLC

    Think of a process you repeat. Could you write its steps down the way you did on Day 5?
