## Core Idea

Backlogs rot when the cost of keeping an idea alive is lower than the cost of
deciding about it.

## Draft

How deep is your backlog? Most teams keep two or three sprints of ready work.
Enough to avoid starvation. Not so much it goes stale.

Then AI arrived. Implementation got cheap. The queue did not get shorter. It got
longer, because writing the ticket now costs more than building the thing.

So the bottleneck moved. It used to be building. Now it is deciding.

Drucker said it plainly: what one postpones, one abandons. A backlog is a list of
postponed decisions. Every item you keep is a decision you have not made.

Delete half of it this week. See what you miss. [[Slow Productivity]] makes the same case.
